## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an athletic yet grounded identity for GRPD, a Moroccan social running and walking platform. It merges the punchy metric legibility of heavy strength and performance tools with the calm, human warmth of Mediterranean and Maghrebi outdoor culture.

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The system rejects aggressive neon palettes and generic gamification. Instead, it balances:
- **Quiet Canvas:** Warm sun-washed off-whites and natural stone surfaces evoking coastal breeze, cedar forests, and limestone trails.
- **Typographic Precision:** High-contrast weights and tabular figures delivering immediate readability mid-stride.
- **Botanical Energy:** Deep cedar greens and crisp mint highlights serving as functional athletic accents rather than pervasive decoration.
- **Restraint and Tactility:** Subtle paper-thin borders, gentle tonal elevation, and tactile pill containers designed for glanceable mobile interaction.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for high daylight legibility under intense sunlight across Moroccan urban boulevards and off-road trails.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#167A47` - Atlas Cedar):** Core action color. Applied to primary CTAs, active telemetry indicators, selected states, and core progression arcs.
- **Secondary (`#1BB367` - Mint Sprint):** Dynamic accent used sparingly for pace acceleration, PR badges, and positive social encouragement indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#0E5832` - Deep Evergreen):** Deep foundational green for high-emphasis metric containers, header contrast chips, and filled status tags.
- **Neutral Dark (`#181A1B` - Charcoal Slate):** Primary reading color for text, run statistics, and core navigational icons. Never pure black (`#000000`).
- **Neutral Secondary (`#33383F` - Basalt):** Secondary hierarchy for metric units (km, bpm, /km), secondary metadata, and subtitles.
- **Neutral Muted (`#6A717B` - Mineral Grey):** Timestamp metadata, inactive tab indicators, disabled states, and grid divider strokes.
- **Canvas Base (`#F9F8F6` - Calcaire):** Warm off-white primary screen backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Chalk):** Elevated stat cards, bottom sheets, and modal views.
- **Surface Subdued (`#F4F2EE` - Sable):** Inset stats, counter blocks, and table zebra striping.
- **Border Outline (`#E8E5DF` - Mortar):** Structural hairline divider for clean card framing without dense drop shadows.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is split intentionally:
- **Space Grotesk** commands the metrics, activity headlines, split tables, and badge labels. Its geometric clarity and monospaced heritage provide immediate scan value for paces, distances, and elevation gradients.
- **Hanken Grotesk** serves the French editorial and community interface (feed descriptions, community announcements, route guides, and settings). It provides smooth, open apertures and high legibility at dense mobile paragraphs.

### Special Formatting Rules
- Always activate tabular numbers (`tnum`) across all telemetry, splits, durations, and heart rate readings to prevent UI horizontal shifting during live tracking.
- Units (`KM`, `/KM`, `BPM`, `D+`, `M`) must be rendered in `label-md` or `label-sm` with 60% neutral opacity directly paired with primary metric values.
- Capitalization: Button labels and section category labels follow sentence casing or strict small caps (`label-sm`).

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined, dense 4pt/8pt spacing scale modeled for one-handed mobile grip and rapid feed consumption.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Mobile Standard (Canvas &lt; 480px):** Single-column stack with `1rem` (16px) outer side margins and an 8px grid baseline. Live workout display adopts a 2x2 or 3x2 metric grid with an internal `gutter` of `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Tablet / Split Views (&gt; 480px):** 6-column fluid structure, `1.5rem` (24px) side margins, pairing interactive map views with persistent stat telemetry drawers.
- **Safe Zones:** Top app bars reserve 48px baseline + device status bar height. Sticky bottom CTA bars adhere to a strict 64px touch height with dynamic home indicator padding.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating drop shadows and blur layers. Depth is constructed strictly through tonal contrast, structured border wraps, and card stepping.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Screen Base):** Background canvas rendered in `#F9F8F6`.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` fills bounded by a 1px solid `#E8E5DF` hairline stroke. No drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Recessed Metrics & Inset Blocks):** `#F4F2EE` fill nestled inside Level 1 cards with zero stroke, signaling data-only containers (e.g., split times, heart rate zones).
- **Level 3 (Interactive Modals & Action Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` surface with an ultra-subdued diffuse grounding shadow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(24, 26, 27, 0.06)`, framed by `#E8E5DF` border on top and lateral axes.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, intentional rounding. Corners are organic and accessible without drifting into childlike bubble forms.

### Corner Radius System
- **Micro UI (`4px`):** Bar chart columns, progress bars, split segments.
- **Input Fields & Small Cards (`8px`):** Text inputs, segment cells, compact chips.
- **Standard Cards & Sheets (`16px`):** Feed items, workout telemetry cards, bottom sheet containers.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Primary action buttons, community tag badges, segment counters, profile avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Button:** High-contrast full pill (`9999px`), background `#167A47`, text `#FFFFFF` in `label-lg`. 52px height for thumb targets. Active state: `#0E5832`.
- **Secondary Surface Button:** Pill shape, background `#F4F2EE`, border 1px solid `#E8E5DF`, text `#181A1B`. Active state: `#E8E5DF`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Button:** Transparent background, text `#167A47` or `#33383F`, underline offset 4px on interaction.

### Badges & Chips
- **Pace / Segment Pill:** `#0E5832` background with `#1BB367` text for elite or high-effort markers.
- **Plein Air / Filter Chips:** Height 32px, pill shape, `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px `#E8E5DF` stroke, inactive text `#6A717B`. Selected state: `#167A47` fill with white text.

### Stat Metrics Cards (Hevy-Inspired Density)
- Exterior card: `#FFFFFF` fill, 16px radius, 1px `#E8E5DF` stroke, `1rem` padding.
- Internal Metric Tiles: 2-column or 3-column grid of `#F4F2EE` containers, 8px radius, 8px padding.
- Values set in `stat-metric` (`Space Grotesk`, `#181A1B`), accompanied by `label-sm` labels (`#6A717B`, uppercase, right- or top-aligned).

### Activity & Community Feed Items
- Profile avatar (40px circular) aligned with runner handle and French localization (`"a couru avec le groupe Atlas"`, `"Hier à 07:14"`).
- Route preview container: 3:2 ratio, muted warm map styling, track polyline highlighted in `#167A47` (2.5px weight).
- Bottom social ribbon: Minimal counter pills for kudos/encouragements (`"Bravo"`, `"Fière"`) with clean stroke icons.

### Inputs & Toggles
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, `#FFFFFF` background, 8px radius, border 1px `#E8E5DF`, focus border 1.5px `#167A47`. Typography: `body-md` in `#181A1B`, placeholder in `#6A717B`.
- **Segmented Controls:** `#F4F2EE` container with a sliding `#FFFFFF` pill thumb, 1px `#E8E5DF` outline on active segment, zero box shadows.
